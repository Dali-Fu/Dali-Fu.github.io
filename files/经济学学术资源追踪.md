## 学术期刊

### 【国际】经济学TOP5

- **AER**  [American Economic Review](https://www.aeaweb.org/articles)：综合顶刊，宏观理论、政策、实证全覆盖，**代码齐全**

- **ECTA**  [Econometrica](https://www.econometricsociety.org/publications/econometrica):理论宏观、计量方法、动态一般均衡，**代码齐全**

- **QJE**  [Quarterly Journal of Economics](https://academic.oup.com/qje):宏观、增长、政治经济、政策评估，**代码齐全**

- **JPE**  [Journal of Political Economy](https://www.journals.uchicago.edu/jpe):宏观机制、结构模型、微观基础，**代码齐全**

- **RES**  [Review of Economic Studies](https://academic.oup.com/restud):宏观理论、动态模型、前沿方法，**代码齐全**

### 【国际】宏观经济领域 Field TOP

- **AEJ: Macro**  [American Economic Journal: Macroeconomics](https://www.aeaweb.org/journals/aej-macro):货币、周期、财政、DSGE、HANK、宏观政策，**代码齐全**

- **JME**  [Journal of Monetary Economics](https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/journal-of-monetary-economics):货币银行、宏观金融、政策传导、金融稳定

- **RED**  [Review of Economic Dynamics](https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/review-of-economic-dynamics):动态宏观、增长、HANK、计算宏观，**代码齐全**

- **JEDC**  [Journal of Economic Dynamics and Control](https://www.journals.elsevier.com/journal-of-economic-dynamics-and-control):DSGE、数值方法、仿真、动态优化

- **JMCB**  [Journal of Money, Credit and Banking](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/journal/14682360):货币、信贷、银行、宏观审慎政策

- **REStat**  [Review of Economics and Statistics](https://direct.mit.edu/rest):实证宏观、因果识别、宏观计量，**代码齐全**

- **EJ**  [The Economic Journal](https://academic.oup.com/ej):欧洲综合顶刊，宏观政策、应用宏观

- **JAE**  [Journal of Applied Econometrics](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/journal/10991255):宏观计量、时间序列、结构估计，**代码齐全**

- **EER**  [European Economic Review](https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/european-economic-review):欧洲宏观、国际宏观、开放经济

- **IER**  [International Economic Review](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/journal/14682354)

- [Macroeconomic Dynamics](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/macroeconomic-dynamics)

- [Journal of Macroeconomics](https://ideas.repec.org/s/eee/jmacro.html)
